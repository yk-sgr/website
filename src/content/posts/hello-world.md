I started this blog because I needed somewhere to think out loud.

Most of what I do sits at the seam between two worlds: the software I build and the business I run. Each one teaches me things I can&rsquo;t learn from the other. Writing both down in the same place &mdash; in front of the same readers &mdash; is part of the point.

You can expect short notes more often than long essays. Code more often than career advice. Specifics more often than generalities. And the occasional detour into whatever I happen to be reading.

If any of that sounds useful, the [RSS feed](/rss.xml) is the best way to follow along, or you can see [more about me](/about/).
